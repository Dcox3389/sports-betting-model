The catch is the payout column. A six-leg college football parlay clears 80% — and pays 1.23x. Fifty dollars returns about sixty-two. The win rate is real; the money is not, because the market prices those favourites correctly.

The vig compounds at roughly 4% per leg. That is parlays in one line: one leg is −4.1%, six legs −22.1%, ten legs −34.1%. Every leg multiplies expected value downward.

Not betting advice. Graded against real closing moneylines these picks return −5.5% per bet. The model predicts winners well and does not beat prices — those are separate skills.
