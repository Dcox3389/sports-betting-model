[T1] Are ESPN odds actually CLOSING odds?
    VERDICT: UNPROVEN - claim overstated
    n=1295 priced games, median hold 4.25%
    share of games with hold < 2% (sharp/late pricing): 0.0%
    ESPN exposes ONE snapshot per game with no timestamp. A 4-3% hold is
    typical of mainline pricing generally -- it does NOT identify the
    snapshot as the close. If these are OPENING numbers, the -5.5% ROI is
    measured against a softer line than a bettor would actually face at
    kickoff, which would make the real result WORSE, not better.

[T2] Is odds coverage biased?
    VERDICT: CONCERN - skewed by league
    priced 1295 of 1838 (70%); 543 missing
    avg margin  priced 13.12  vs  unpriced 12.45
    coverage nba     80%  (1065 of 1334)
    coverage wnba    46%  (230 of 504)
    spread between leagues: 34%
    The market test is weighted toward whichever league is best covered
    and speaks correspondingly less for the others.

[T3] Does the devig method drive the result?
    VERDICT: PASS - robust to method
    mean (actual - fair) by devig method:
      proportional  -0.0079
      power         -0.0109
      additive      -0.0098
    spread across methods: 0.0030
    All three stay negative, so 'no edge' is not an artifact of one
    devig choice. Proportional is known to overstate favourites, so
    it is the most GENEROUS to our favourite-heavy picks -- and it
    still shows no edge.

[T4] Is the walk-forward genuinely leak-free?
    VERDICT: PASS - truncation invariant
    graded 2025-10-18 twice from the cached CFB season: once with all
    2889 games, once with every game after that date removed
    picks compared: 48   differing confidences: 0
    identical to 9 decimal places -- future games provably cannot
    influence a prediction dated before them

[T5] Do parlays assume false independence?
    VERDICT: PASS - near zero
    same-day same-league pick pairs: n=6265, r=-0.0028
    cross-day control pairs:        n=29, r=+nan
    Parlay math multiplies leg probabilities, which assumes independence.
    Positive same-day correlation would make real parlays hit MORE often
    than estimated; negative would make them hit less.

[T6] Was CFB home-field tuned on the holdout?
    VERDICT: CONFIRMED - minor overfit
    NFL home-field (35) was derived from the 55.2% home win rate over the
    WARM-UP seasons only -- legitimate.
    CFB home-field (60) was chosen because it topped a sweep run ON THE
    HELD-OUT SEASON (71.3% at 60 vs 71.1% at 65). That is tuning on the
    holdout, however mild.
    Impact is small -- the sweep spanned 69.5%-71.3% across HFA 0-100, so
    the parameter barely matters -- but the CFB accuracy figure is very
    slightly optimistic and should not be quoted to a tenth of a point.

[T7] Is 84% a threshold-shopping artifact?
    VERDICT: MOSTLY PASS
    accuracy is monotone in the threshold, not a spike at 85%:
      >= 80.0%  n=1877   acc 79.4%
      >= 82.5%  n=1396   acc 82.0%
      >= 85.0%  n=995    acc 84.0%
      >= 87.5%  n=691    acc 85.4%
      >= 90.0%  n=464    acc 86.9%
    A cherry-picked threshold shows a bump that vanishes on either
    side. This rises smoothly, which is what a real effect does.
    The 85% CUTOFF was still chosen after seeing the data, so the
    84% figure carries selection risk even though the trend is real.
